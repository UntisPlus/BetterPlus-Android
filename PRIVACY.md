# Privacy Policy

**UntisPlus** — Android client for the Untis timetable system
Last updated: 2026-08-23

## The short version

UntisPlus does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on servers
operated by the developer. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no
tracking of any kind built into the app.

## Data the app handles

- **Your Untis credentials** (username and an app-specific access key) are
  entered by you and stored **only locally on your device**, in the app's
  private storage. They are transmitted exclusively to the Untis server your
  school provides (or to an API endpoint you explicitly configure yourself,
  such as a self-hosted proxy).
- **Timetable and school data** (your timetable, absences, messages, etc.)
  is fetched from your school's Untis server and cached **locally on your
  device**. It is never sent anywhere else.
- **Notifications and auto-mute features** run locally on your device using
  your timetable data; they do not involve external services.

## Permissions

The app requests only the permissions needed for its features (for example:
notifications, network access, optionally camera for QR-code profile import).
None of them are used to collect information about you.

## Third parties

UntisPlus contains no third-party tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.
Communication happens solely between your device and your school's Untis
infrastructure (or the alternative endpoint you configure).

## Untis itself

This app is an unofficial client. Your use of Untis is additionally governed
by your school's and Untis GmbH's own policies, which are independent of this
project.

## Contact

Open an issue at https://github.com/UntisPlus/UntisPlus-Android/issues if
you have questions about this policy.
