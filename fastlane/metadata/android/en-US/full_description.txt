UntisPlus is an open-source Android client for the Untis timetable system,
built as a hard fork of BetterUntis with its own identity, so it can be
installed and used side-by-side with the original app.

View your timetable with a modern Material 3 interface built on Jetpack
Compose. Customize almost any aspect of it, including multiple themes,
colors and dark mode.

Easily add multiple profiles, each with separate settings, credentials and
timetables. Smart caching keeps previously loaded weeks available offline
and loads updates fast in the background.

Protect your privacy: UntisPlus is fully free and open source software,
contains no tracking or advertising, works without Google Play Services,
and supports custom API endpoints including self-hosted proxies.

Other features include:
- Room finder to quickly search for free rooms at your school
- Info center with events, messages of the day, exams and office hours
- Widgets for your home screen
- Break notifications and auto-mute during lessons
- Teacher features such as absence checking and lesson topic editing

Note: Untis is proprietary timetable software used by your school. To use
this app, your school must provide Untis and an account for it, unless
anonymous access is available.
